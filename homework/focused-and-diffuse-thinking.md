# Q1 Explain
### What is the difference between focused mode and diffuse mode? Explain in your own words.

Focused mode is when you are thinking of one specific question, and diffuse mode is more like wandering without thinking any question.

# Q2 Apply
### Can the same activity involve either mode at different times? Give one activity and describe two different ways of directing your attention.

Yes, for example, when you sit on a chair doing math problem and when you sit on a chair and for relax is different. For the first situation, you have a question to think about, and for the second question, you do not really have a question, so you do not focus on anything.

# Q3 Transfer
### You are studying for a difficult exam and become stuck on a problem. How would you decide whether to keep working, change strategy, or take a break? Explain your reason and what you would do when you return.

This is depending on how many methods you know, obviously you cannot take a break during an exam, so if you know other methods, you should try other methods to solve the problem. If you do not know other methods and very sure the previous method really does not work, then you can skip the question and do other questions, you might go back when finish other questions.
