| Method | What It Is | Best Used When | Strengths | Limitations | Type of Data Collected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Naturalistic Observation | observing people in their natural context | want to see what you want to see | simply watch | can't interact with users directly and can't capture identifying information and don't know anything about what those users are thinking | people's behaviors |
| Participant Observation | experience a task for ourselves | use when experience as a participant observer | feel it by yourself | participants might lie | notes |
| Interviews & Focus Groups | most targeted way of gather information from users | want to explore user's experience | more deep level than observation | people more likely to agree with you instead of bringing new ideas | open ended answers |
| Think-Aloud | let users to talk about their perceptions of the task | want to record user's thoughts | get at users thoughts that they forget when they are no longer engaged in the task | change the way they actually act | open ended answers |
| Surveys | multiple choice question | when you want quickly get information | save times compare to other method | the answers aren't deep | multiple choice |
