## 1. What the recommended order of brainstorming, individual --> group or vice versa and why?

Individual --> group, because you need to think your idea first then share your idea with others. If you work in group first, you might loss your own idea
  
## 2. Write your 5 tips for individual brainstorming with explanations in your own words

1. Write down the main question, so all the steps next are around this topic
2. Force yourself to think all the solution, even it seems not too realistic
3. Don't stop thinking until you got 20 ideas
4. Take a break, after the break you might found other ideas
5. Divide the big ideas into smaller one, so it is easier for you to think about each solution
 
## 3. What are the challenges we might come across in group brainstorming?

People might work less in group than individual
People tend to agree other's opinion 
There might be a leader, and other's idea could be ignored
The momentum for working might reduce overtime

## 4. List the tips for group brainstorming

Don't make the group too large (recommend no more than 5 people)
Set clear goal, what is the goal of the brainstorming
Let everyone in the group share their own idea
Make rules for sharing, like time limit for each team member
The final result should be ends with idea not a decision

## 5. How do the tips of both individual and group brainstorming help deal with the challenges for brainstorming?

In individual brainstorming, tips help us to think wider, for example: sometimes you might think it is not too realistic, so you don't consider that idea. In group brainstorming, tips give opportunity for every team member to share their own idea instead of only 1 member share the idea and everyone else tend to agree.
