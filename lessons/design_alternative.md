## 1. What the recommended order of brainstorming, individual --> group or vice versa and why?

Individual --> group, because you need to think your idea first then share your idea with others. If you work in group first, you might loss your own idea
  
## 2. Write your 5 tips for individual brainstorming with explanations in your own words

1. Write down the main question, so all the steps next are around this topic
2. Force yourself to think all the solution, even it seems not too realistic
3. Don't stop thinking until you got 20 ideas
4. Take a break, after the break you might found other ideas
5. Divide the big ideas into smaller one, so it is easier for you to think about each solution
